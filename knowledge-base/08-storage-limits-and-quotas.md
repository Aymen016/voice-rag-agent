# Storage Limits and Quotas

This document explains how storage is counted, the size limits for individual files, and what happens when an account runs out of space.

## What counts toward storage

Every file in the account counts toward the quota, including files inside folders shared with others. Earlier versions of files and items in the Recycle Bin do not count.

## Maximum file size

The largest single file that can be uploaded is 2 GB on Free, 50 GB on Pro, and 100 GB on Business. Larger files must be split or compressed before uploading.

## When the account is full

At 90% of the quota, CloudNest shows a warning in the apps and sends an email. At 100%:

- New uploads and edits from desktop and mobile apps stop syncing to the cloud.
- Existing files remain fully available to view, download and share.
- Nothing is deleted.

Sync resumes automatically once usage drops below the limit, either by removing files or upgrading.

## Adding more space

Free customers can upgrade to Pro for 2 TB. Pro customers who need more than 2 TB should move to Business. CloudNest does not sell standalone storage add-ons.

## Business pooled storage

On Business, storage is pooled across the team: each seat adds 5 TB to a shared pool that any member can use. Admins can set per-member limits from the admin console.
