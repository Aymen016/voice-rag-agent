# Support and Service Levels

This document describes how to reach CloudNest support, how quickly each plan can expect a reply, and the uptime commitment for Business.

## Support channels

All customers can reach support through the in-app help center or by voice and chat with the virtual support agent, which can open tickets on the customer's behalf. Business customers can also call phone support on weekdays.

## Response time targets

The first reply to a support ticket is targeted within:

- **Free**: 3 business days, by email.
- **Pro**: 24 hours, every day of the week.
- **Business**: 4 business hours; urgent tickets marked as a full outage are answered within 1 hour at any time.

These are targets for the first response, not for full resolution.

## Ticket priorities

Tickets are triaged as low, normal, high or urgent. Urgent is reserved for situations where the customer cannot access any files or suspects their account was compromised. Billing disputes and refund requests are normally handled as normal priority by the billing team.

## Uptime commitment

CloudNest commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for Business customers. If uptime falls below that level in a month, Business customers receive a service credit of 10% of that month's fee, applied to the next invoice. Credits are requested through a billing ticket within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Free and Pro plans do not include an uptime commitment.

## Status page

Current incidents and planned maintenance are published on the CloudNest status page. Maintenance windows are announced at least 72 hours in advance and are scheduled outside 08:00 to 20:00 UTC on weekdays.
