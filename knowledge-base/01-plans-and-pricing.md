# Plans and Pricing

CloudNest is a cloud file storage, backup and sync service for individuals and teams. This document describes the subscription tiers, what each one includes, and how pricing works.

## Available plans

CloudNest offers three tiers:

- **Free**: 5 GB of storage, sync on up to 2 devices, file version history for 7 days, and email support.
- **Pro**: 2 TB of storage, sync on unlimited devices, 180 days of version history, password-protected share links, and priority support.
- **Business**: 5 TB of storage per seat, everything in Pro, an admin console, single sign-on (SSO), audit logs, and phone support. Business requires a minimum of 3 seats.

## Prices

Pro costs $12 per month when billed monthly, or $120 per year when billed annually (equivalent to two months free).

Business costs $20 per seat per month when billed monthly, or $192 per seat per year when billed annually.

Prices are listed in US dollars and exclude local sales tax or VAT, which is added at checkout based on the billing address.

## Discounts and special pricing

Registered nonprofit organizations can request 30% off the Business plan by submitting proof of nonprofit status to the billing team. This discount does not apply to Pro.

Teams purchasing 50 or more Business seats can contact sales for volume pricing.

CloudNest does not offer a lifetime plan or one-time purchase option. All paid plans are subscriptions.

## Free trial

New accounts can try Pro free for 14 days. No payment method is needed to start the trial. When the trial ends, the account returns to the Free plan unless a payment method has been added.

## Upgrading and downgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately. The customer is charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period.

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. If the stored data exceeds the lower plan's quota after a downgrade, the account becomes read-only for uploads until usage is reduced (see Storage Limits and Quotas).
