# Billing and Payments

This document explains how CloudNest charges customers, which payment methods are accepted, and what happens when a payment fails.

## Billing cycle

Subscriptions renew automatically on the same calendar day each month (monthly billing) or each year (annual billing). The renewal date is shown under Settings > Billing. An invoice is emailed to the account owner after every successful charge.

## Accepted payment methods

CloudNest accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Business customers paying for 20 or more seats annually can request payment by bank transfer against an invoice.

CloudNest does not accept cryptocurrency, prepaid gift cards, or cash payments.

## Updating payment details

The account owner can change the card or PayPal account at any time under Settings > Billing > Payment method. The new method is used for the next renewal. Changing the payment method does not trigger a new charge.

## Failed payments

If a renewal charge fails, CloudNest retries the payment method three times over 7 days and emails the account owner after each attempt. If all retries fail, the account is moved to the Free plan. Files are never deleted because of a failed payment, but uploads are blocked while usage is above the Free quota of 5 GB.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges

Occasionally a payment processor may submit the same renewal twice, which shows up as two identical charges on the same day. Customers who see two charges for the same subscription period should contact billing support with the invoice numbers. Confirmed duplicate charges are always refunded in full (see Refund and Cancellation Policy).

A charge the customer does not recognize may also be a prorated upgrade charge or sales tax added at checkout. The invoice description identifies which it is.

## Invoices and receipts

All invoices are available under Settings > Billing > Invoice history and can be downloaded as PDF. Business admins can add a company name, address and tax ID that will appear on future invoices. Past invoices cannot be reissued with different details.
