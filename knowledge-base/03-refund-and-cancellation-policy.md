# Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy describes when CloudNest returns money to customers and how cancellations work. Refunds are reviewed and processed by the billing team; the support agent can check eligibility and open a billing ticket but cannot issue money directly.

## Cooling-off period

Any new paid charge, monthly or annual, can be reimbursed in full if the customer asks within 14 days of the charge date, for any reason. This applies once per account per plan.

## Monthly subscriptions after 14 days

Monthly charges older than 14 days are not reimbursed. The customer can cancel at any time to stop the next renewal, and keeps paid features until the end of the period they already paid for.

## Annual subscriptions after 14 days

For annual plans, a partial reimbursement is available between day 15 and day 30 after the charge. The amount is calculated for the unused full months: the annual price multiplied by the number of unused months, divided by 12. Any partial month that has started counts as used.

Annual charges older than 30 days are not eligible for any reimbursement. The plan remains active until the end of the paid year.

## Duplicate charges

If the same subscription period was charged twice, the duplicate charge is always reimbursed in full, as long as it is reported within 120 days. This does not count against the one-time cooling-off allowance.

## Service outages

Outages do not qualify for cash refunds. Business customers may instead receive service credits under the uptime commitment (see Support and Service Levels).

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank. Refunds cannot be sent to a different card or account.

## Cancelling a subscription

The account owner can cancel under Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not by itself trigger a refund. After the paid period ends the account moves to the Free plan, and no files are deleted at that point.
