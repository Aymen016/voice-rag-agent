# Sharing and Permissions

CloudNest lets customers give other people access to files and folders, either by inviting them directly or by creating a link.

## Inviting people

Any file or folder can be shared with specific people by email. Each person gets one of three roles:

- **Viewer**: can open and download.
- **Commenter**: can view and leave comments.
- **Editor**: can view, comment, upload, rename, move and delete inside the shared folder.

People invited do not need a paid plan. Files in a shared folder count against the storage of the owner, not the people it is shared with.

## Share links

A share link gives access to anyone who has the link. On the Free plan, links are view-only and never expire. Pro and Business customers can additionally set a password on a link, set an expiry date, and disable downloads so that files can only be previewed in the browser.

## Revoking access

To stop sharing, open the item's Share panel and remove the person or delete the link. Access ends immediately, including for anyone who already had the link open, at their next action.

Copies that someone already downloaded to their own device cannot be recalled.

## Transferring ownership

Ownership of a folder can be transferred to another CloudNest user, who must accept the transfer. Business admins can transfer all files of a departing team member to another member from the admin console.

## Business sharing controls

Business admins can restrict sharing outside the organization, require passwords on all external links, and set a maximum link expiry period for the whole team.
