# Privacy and Data Location

This document explains where CloudNest stores customer data and how it handles privacy requests.

## Data centers

Customer files are stored in one of two regions: the United States or the European Union (Frankfurt and Dublin). The region is chosen when the account is created, based on the sign-up country. Business customers can choose the region when setting up their organization.

Moving an existing account to a different region is available to Business customers only and is done by the support team; it can take up to 7 days depending on data volume.

## Who can access customer data

CloudNest employees cannot open customer files. During a support case, the customer can grant temporary, time-limited access to a specific folder, which expires automatically after 72 hours.

CloudNest does not sell customer data and does not use file contents to train machine learning models.

## Privacy requests

Customers can request a copy of the personal data CloudNest holds about them (separate from their files) or ask for it to be deleted. Requests are submitted through a support ticket in the "account" category and are completed within 30 days.

## Compliance

CloudNest is GDPR-compliant and offers a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) to Business customers, available in the admin console.
