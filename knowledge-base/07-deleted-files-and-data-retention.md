# Deleted Files, Version History and Data Retention

This document explains what happens to removed files, how earlier versions are kept, and what happens to data when an account is closed.

## Recycle Bin

When a file or folder is removed, it moves to the Recycle Bin instead of being erased. Items stay in the Recycle Bin for 30 days on every plan, and can be restored to their original location from the web app. Items in the Recycle Bin do not count against the storage quota.

After 30 days, items in the Recycle Bin are permanently erased and cannot be recovered by support.

Emptying the Recycle Bin manually erases its contents immediately and permanently.

## Version history

CloudNest keeps earlier versions of a file each time it changes. The retention window depends on the plan: 7 days on Free and 180 days on Pro and Business. Any earlier version can be restored from the file's History panel. Restoring a version creates a new version, so nothing is lost.

## Closing an account

The account owner can close the account under Settings > Account > Close account. Paid subscriptions must be cancelled first.

After closure there is a 30-day grace period during which the customer can sign in and reopen the account with all files intact. After the grace period, all files, versions and share links are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

## Inactive Free accounts

Free accounts with no sign-in for 24 months are considered inactive. CloudNest sends three warning emails over 60 days before deleting the files of an inactive account. Signing in at any time resets the inactivity period.

## Exporting data

Customers can download all of their files at any time from Settings > Account > Export data, which produces a set of ZIP archives emailed as download links within 48 hours.
